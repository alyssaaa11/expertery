Millimetres, Z up. L-bracket with a cable notch. Base 60 (X) by 40 (Y), 5 thick. Upright 60 wide, 5 thick, 45 tall overall, standing on the back edge of the base and flush with it. A rectangular cable notch, 10 wide and 15 deep, is cut down from the top edge of the upright in the middle of its length. Two 5.5 mm holes go through the base, 10 from each side and 15 from the front edge; two 5.5 mm holes go through the upright along Y, 10 from each side and 30 above the bottom. Placement: base corner on the origin, X from 0 to 60, front edge on Y = 0, bottom on Z = 0.
